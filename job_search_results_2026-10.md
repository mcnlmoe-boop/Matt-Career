# Live Job Search Results: October 26, 2026

Sources: Indeed job search plus web searches. Indeed returned thin results for
niche component-engineering terms, so finding no postings for a company does not
mean that company isn't hiring. Check career sites directly for Generac, Sensata,
TE, Eaton, Itron, Landis+Gyr and Constellation.

## What the search changed

1. **Nuclear and architect-engineering (A/E) firms moved to #1.** They had the most
   concrete postings, the best pay evidence, and the closest locations. Industry
   reports confirm a structural shortage of experienced nuclear engineers.
2. **DC contactors dropped from #1 to a targeted, not volume, track.** The market is
   real but small: about $585M in 2026, growing roughly 7–10% a year, according to
   market-research estimates that disagree with each other. No openings surfaced.
   Gigavac's contactor center is in Carpinteria, CA, so this track likely means
   relocating.
3. **The degree risk appeared in a real posting.** A 2024 Constellation electrical
   engineer posting asked for a BS in Electrical Engineering *or* a PE. A PE would
   close the gap left by your BS in Electrical Engineering Technology.
